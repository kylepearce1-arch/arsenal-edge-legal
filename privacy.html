Arsenal Edge

Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

Arsenal Edge is provided by Aplomb Financial LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Arsenal Edge mobile app collects, why, who else can see it, and how to get rid of it.

We have tried to make this specific rather than generic. Where we say we do not collect something, we mean the app contains no code that collects it.

Questions: Kyle@aplombfinancial.com

The short version

  • We collect the email address you sign up with, the bowler profile you fill in, and the bowling data you record.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or camera. The app requests no device permissions at all.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no advertising identifier. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • Pinpoint sends bowling context and your typed question to a third-party AI provider so it can answer. It does not send your name, email, or account identifier.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all of its data from inside the app.

1. What we collect

Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address, and Supabase (our hosting provider) stores your password in hashed form. We never see, store, or log your password ourselves. We also keep the timestamps Supabase records, such as when your account was created and confirmed.

Bowler profile. Whatever you choose to enter: handedness, average range, delivery style, ball speed, and rev rate. All of it is optional and you can edit it at any time.

Your bowling data. This is the substance of the app:

  • Sessions: the bowling center name, lane pair, oil pattern, session type, number of bowlers on the pair, and your notes.
  • Games, frames, and shots: scores, pinfall, strikes, spares, splits, washouts, fouls, which pins were left standing, spare miss direction, and the line you played.
  • Your arsenal: which balls you own, plus your own layout description, surface, reaction notes, and general notes.
  • Leagues you save, oil patterns you create, and your practice focuses, drills, and attempts, including execution notes.

Several of these are free-text fields. Whatever you type into them is stored as you typed it, so please avoid putting sensitive personal information in a notes field.

Pinpoint usage counter. We count how many Pinpoint requests you make each day, to enforce a daily limit. We store the count and the date. We do not store your messages.

Local diagnostics. If the app crashes, it saves the error name, message, and stack trace on your device, keeping at most the ten most recent. This never leaves your phone automatically. It contains no profile, account, or bowling data. The only way it reaches us is if you tap "Email support" after a crash, which opens a draft in your own mail app containing the error text — you decide whether to send it.

2. What we do not collect

The app declares no Android or iOS permissions, and the following are absent from the app entirely — not merely unused, but not present in the software:

  • Location, GPS, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, and calendar
  • Advertising identifiers, device fingerprints, and any persistent hardware identifier
  • Analytics, telemetry, attribution, and crash-reporting SDKs
  • Advertising networks
  • Push notifications
  • Social or third-party sign-in

The app makes network requests to exactly one destination: our Supabase backend.

3. How Pinpoint works

Pinpoint is the app's coaching assistant. It runs on a third-party AI provider — currently Google's Gemini API — which we call from our server, never from your phone.

What is sent to the AI provider when you ask Pinpoint something:

  • Your typed message, and up to your last ten messages in the conversation
  • Your bowler profile attributes: handedness, average range, delivery style, ball speed, rev rate
  • Up to twenty balls from your arsenal, including your own free-text layout, surface, and reaction notes
  • In general chat, statistics we calculate from up to your 500 most recent completed games — averages, strike and spare rates, common leaves, per-ball performance, the date range of your play history, and averages grouped by bowling center and oil pattern
  • During live scoring, your current session context: center, lane pair, pattern, game and frame number, current ball, and recent leaves
  • Your active practice focus

What is never sent to the AI provider: your name, your email address, your account identifier, your login token, and the free-text notes attached to execution markers. The request carries no identifier that ties it back to you.

What we do with the conversation: nothing. We do not store Pinpoint conversations, on our servers or on your device. They live in the app's memory while the screen is open and are gone when you leave it. Our server logs record only technical metrics such as the model used and token counts — never your messages.

How the AI provider handles it. We use the provider's paid tier, and under its terms that means it does not use your prompts or responses to train or improve its models. It does log prompts and responses for a limited period, solely to detect abuse of its service and to meet legal obligations, and that logged data may be stored or cached in any country where the provider operates. For this content the provider acts as our data processor.

If you would still rather a piece of information not be processed this way, do not put it in a note field or a Pinpoint message.

4. Who else sees your data

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising. We use these service providers:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and server functions. All of your account and bowling data is stored there. Like any hosting provider, Supabase processes technical information such as IP addresses and request metadata in its platform logs.
  • Google (Gemini API) — the AI provider described in Section 3.
  • Our email provider — sends account confirmation and password reset emails, which necessarily includes your email address.
  • Google Play — distributes the app. Google collects its own data about installs independently of us, governed by Google's privacy policy.

Nothing you create in Arsenal Edge is visible to other users. There is no sharing, no social feed, and no public profile. Your data is readable only by your own account, which we enforce at the database level.

We may also disclose data if we are legally required to, or to protect the safety and rights of our users.

5. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from Account & Settings → Delete account. You will be asked to type DELETE to confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. There is no grace period, no recovery window, and no way for us to restore a deleted account. It removes your login, your profile, your sessions, games, frames, and shots, your arsenal, your leagues, your custom oil patterns, your practice history, and your Pinpoint usage counters. The app also wipes the bowling data stored on your device, including anything not yet synced.

Two things do not disappear at that instant. Our hosting provider's platform logs and routine backups may retain records for a limited period before aging out, and the local crash log stays on your device because it contains no account or bowling data — deleting the app removes it.

If you cannot access the app, see the account deletion request page.

6. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your profile and arsenal at any time in the app, and delete individual sessions, games, and balls. Deleting your account removes everything.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these, email us at Kyle@aplombfinancial.com.

We want to be straightforward about one limitation: the app currently has no data export feature. If you ask us for a copy of your data we will assemble it manually.

7. How we protect your data

Every table in our database enforces row-level security, so your rows are readable only by your authenticated account. Your login session is stored in your device's secure keystore rather than ordinary app storage. Traffic to our servers uses HTTPS. Our privileged server credentials and AI provider key exist only on the server and are never shipped in the app.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Arsenal Edge is intended for adults. You must be at least 18 to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

9. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists, because the app's value is the history it accumulates. When you delete your account, it goes immediately, subject to the provider log and backup windows described in Section 5.

10. International users

We operate from the United States and your account and bowling data are processed there. If you use Arsenal Edge from outside the United States, you are sending your information to the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your country. Separately, the abuse-detection logs our AI provider keeps of Pinpoint requests may be stored or cached in other countries where that provider operates, as described in Section 3.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date at the top, and for significant changes we will make it apparent in the app. Continuing to use Arsenal Edge after a change means you accept the updated policy.
