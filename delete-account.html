Delete your account

Aplomb Financial LLC

You can delete your Arsenal Edge account and everything in it. The fastest way is inside the app; if you cannot get into the app, you can ask us by email.

Option 1 — delete it in the app

  1. Open Arsenal Edge and sign in.
  2. Go to Account & Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Permanently delete account.

The deletion happens immediately.

Option 2 — ask us to delete it

If you have lost access to the app or your device, email Kyle@aplombfinancial.com with the subject Arsenal Edge account deletion request.

Send it from the email address on the account — that is how we confirm the request is really yours. We do not ask for your password, and you should never send it to us.

We will delete the account and confirm by reply, normally within 7 days and no later than 30 days.

This cannot be undone

There is no grace period and no recovery window. Once an account is deleted we cannot restore it, and neither can you. If you want to keep a record of your scores or statistics, write them down before you delete — the app has no export feature.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account, permanently:

  • Your login and email address
  • Your bowler profile
  • Every session, game, frame, and shot you have recorded
  • Your arsenal, including your layouts, surfaces, and notes
  • Your saved leagues and any oil patterns you created
  • Your practice focuses, drills, and attempts
  • Your Pinpoint usage counters

Deleting from inside the app also wipes the bowling data stored on your phone, including anything that had not yet synced.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Provider logs and backups. Our hosting provider's platform logs and routine backups may still contain records for a limited period before they age out on their normal schedule. They are not accessible in the app and are not used for anything.
  • The local crash log stays on your device. It holds only error messages and stack traces — no account or bowling data — and uninstalling the app removes it.
  • Pinpoint conversations are never stored in the first place, so there is nothing to delete.
